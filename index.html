Most clinics are built for volume. We built ours for precision and attention.

One of New Gurgaon's first specialist-led multi-speciality dental clinics, The Oral Care Collective offers department-driven dentistry with an unhurried, personalised approach. Our specialists

Consultant Orthodontist

Dr. Subbu Kashyap

BDS, MDS – Orthodontics & Dentofacial Orthopaedics · Certified Invisalign Provider · MBA, IIM Nagpur

Dr. Subbu Kashyap is a specialist Orthodontist dedicated to creating confident, healthy smiles through precise, evidence-based orthodontic care. With expertise in braces and clear aligner therapy, she focuses on delivering beautifully balanced results while preserving long-term oral function and facial aesthetics.

Her clinical interests include adult orthodontics, Invisalign treatment, growth modification in children, and multidisciplinary smile rehabilitation. Dr. Kashyap believes every smile is unique, and her treatment philosophy centres on personalised planning, minimally invasive care, and results that look natural and last.
